---
title: "Privacy and PHI basics"
description: "Handle resident and grievance information carefully while using CareResolve."
---

CareResolve is used for sensitive grievance operations. Users should document enough to support review and resolution, but avoid unnecessary sensitive information.

## Treat as sensitive

Treat the following as sensitive:

- Resident names.
- Room numbers and unit details.
- Grievance narratives.
- Resident words.
- Reporter contact information.
- Audio, images, documents, attachments, or other supporting files.
- Concern-line call recordings and transcripts.
- Resident Council meeting recordings, transcripts, and summaries.
- Resident information shown from PointClickCare.
- Attachment contents.
- Case notes and investigation findings.

## Keep the full record in CareResolve

Do not move PHI into unapproved systems.

Avoid copying grievance narratives, resident identifiers, attachments, or case notes into:

- Support emails unless specifically approved.
- External chat tools.
- Analytics tools.
- Issue trackers.
- Shared documents that are not approved for PHI.

## Support messages

The in-app **Contact support**, **Give feedback**, and **Report a bug** forms ask you to confirm that the message contains no PHI. Describe the problem and the page you were on; refer to a grievance by its ID rather than by resident name.

## Notifications and emails

Notification emails are intentionally brief. They identify the grievance, facility, action, and a link back to CareResolve. The daily summary email contains no resident or narrative details.

Open CareResolve to review the full case record.

## Exports and reports

CSV exports and PDF reports may contain sensitive resident, reporter, and case information. Generated files are not retained in CareResolve, so downloaded copies must be stored and shared through approved systems.

Before sharing an export or report, confirm:

- The facility scope is correct.
- The date range is correct.
- The status and label filters are correct.
- Attachments are included only when needed.
- Recipients are approved to receive the information.

## AI outputs

AI summaries, Assistant answers, and pattern recommendations may reference grievance information.

Use them inside CareResolve as review aids. Do not treat them as final decisions or copy sensitive content into unapproved tools.
