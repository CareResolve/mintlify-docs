---
title: "Import a meeting recording"
description: "Turn a Resident Council recording into a transcript, a draft summary, and suggested grievances to review."
---

If a Resident Council meeting was recorded, import the audio into its meeting record. CareResolve transcribes it, drafts a summary, and suggests concerns that may need a grievance.

AI only drafts. The summary reaches your notes when you add it, and a suggestion becomes a grievance only when a person files it.

## Import the audio

1. Open **Resident Council** and open the meeting.
2. Select **Import a recording**.
3. Choose an M4A, MP3, or WAV file, or drop it into the dialog.
4. Confirm that everyone in the meeting was told it was being recorded.
5. Select **Import**.

Keep the page open until the upload finishes. Transcription then continues in the background; an hour of audio takes about 6 minutes.

<Warning>
  Only import a recording when everyone present was told about it. Follow your facility's policy and state law on recording meetings.
</Warning>

## Review the result

When processing finishes, the meeting page shows:

- **Notes:** your own meeting notes, unchanged.
- **AI summary:** draft minutes generated from the transcript. Select **Add to notes** to copy the summary into your notes, where you can edit it.
- **Transcript:** the full text. Speakers are numbered, not named. Select a passage to play the recording from that moment.
- **Suggested grievances:** concerns found in the recording.

## File or dismiss suggested grievances

Each suggestion shows the concern and a timestamp. Select the timestamp to open the transcript at that quote and hear it.

- **File** creates a Resident Council grievance linked to this meeting, received on the meeting's date, with you as the filer.
- **Dismiss** sets the suggestion aside.

Nothing is filed until you file it. Read the transcript passage first: the suggestion is a prompt for review, not a finding. Like other Resident Council submissions, a filed grievance omits resident and reporter identity.

## Remove a recording

Administrators can open the meeting's action menu and select **Remove recording**. This deletes the audio, the transcript, the summary, and any suggestions that have not been filed. Filed grievances and your notes stay.

If an upload did not finish or a file could not be transcribed, anyone who can edit the meeting can remove it and import the file again. Deleting the meeting also deletes its recording.

## Privacy

Recordings and transcripts can contain resident names and health details. Keep them in CareResolve, and do not paste transcript text into outside tools.
